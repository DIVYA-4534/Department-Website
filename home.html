Welcome to the Department of Commerce

Established at Sri Venkateswara College (Benito Juarez Marg, Dhaula Kuan, New Delhi), the Department of Commerce is one of the most prestigious departments in South Campus, University of Delhi. Guided by the motto "Truth Through Education", the department focuses on academic excellence, practical business exposure, and holistic student development.

About the Department

The Department of Commerce offers undergraduate education under the NEP-UGCF framework of the University of Delhi. Alongside classroom teaching, students actively participate in research programs like SRIVIPRA, corporate internships, and departmental societies such as ComSoc and The Bluechip Society.

Courses Offered

  • B.Com. (Programme): 3/4-year undergraduate degree under NEP-UGCF covering Financial Accounting, Business Laws, Corporate Accounting, and Income Tax.
  • B.Com. (Honours): Specialized honours program in Commerce, Finance, Economics, and Management.

Academic Activities, Seminars & Events

  • Annual Departmental Events: Comverse, Consortium, Arthkumbh, Nivesh, and AlumVision organized by the Commerce Society (ComSoc).
  • Workshops & Seminars: Workshops on Understanding Policy Through Practice, financial literacy drives, and corporate guest lectures.
  • Undergraduate Research (SRIVIPRA): Summer internship and research mentorship projects guided by commerce faculty members.
